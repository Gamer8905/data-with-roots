Use Case 3

Retail Demand Forecasting

ML to optimize inventory and customer satisfaction in retail chains.

Problem Context

Retail chains constantly face the challenge of balancing inventory: having enough stock to meet demand without incurring excessive storage costs. Lack of product availability leads to customer loss, while overstock causes economic losses due to spoilage and obsolescence.

The problem is: How to predict future product demand to optimize purchasing, reduce waste, and maximize customer satisfaction.

🛒

Type of ML

Supervised Learning - Regression

Time Series XGBoost LSTM

Data that could be used

  • Sales history by product and store.
  • Past pricing and promotion data.
  • Seasonal variables and calendar (holidays).
  • External weather data.
  • Competitor pricing and market events.

What the model should predict

  • Demanded quantity: Expected units sold per product.
  • Period: Daily, weekly, or monthly prediction.
  • Alerts: When demand will exceed current stock capacity.

Potential benefit

Reduce losses from overstock and stockouts by 20-30%, improve customer satisfaction by consistently keeping products available, optimize the supply chain, and reduce waste of perishable products.
