Use Case 3

Demand Prediction in Retail

ML to optimize inventory and customer satisfaction in retail chains.

Problem Context

Retail chains face the constant challenge of balancing inventory: having enough stock to meet demand without incurring excessive storage costs. Lack of products leads to customer loss, while overstock leads to economic losses from expiration and obsolescence.

The problem is: How to predict future product demand to optimize purchases, reduce waste and maximize customer satisfaction.

🛒

ML Type

Supervised Learning - Regression

Time Series XGBoost LSTM

Data that could be used

  • Sales history by product and store.
  • Previous price and promotion data.
  • Seasonal variables and calendar (holidays).
  • External weather data.
  • Competitor prices and market events.

What the model should predict

  • Demand quantity: Expected sales units per product.
  • Period: Daily, weekly or monthly prediction.
  • Alerts: When demand will exceed current stock capacity.

Potential benefit

Reduce losses from overstock and stockouts by 20-30%, improve customer satisfaction by always having products available, optimize the supply chain and reduce waste of perishable products.
