Use Case 2

Financial Fraud Detection

ML to identify fraudulent transactions in real-time in the banking sector.

Problem Context

Financial institutions process millions of daily transactions. Credit card fraud costs billions of dollars annually worldwide. Traditional methods based on static rules fail to detect sophisticated and modern fraud patterns.

The problem is: How to identify fraudulent transactions in real-time among millions of legitimate operations, minimizing false positives.

🏦

ML Type

Unsupervised Learning + Supervised

Anomaly Detection Random Forest

Data that could be used

  • Amount and time of each transaction.
  • Geographic location of the point of sale.
  • Customer transaction history.
  • Frequency and usual spending pattern.
  • Device and channel used.

What the model should predict

  • Classification: Whether a transaction is legitimate or fraudulent.
  • Risk score: Fraud probability (0-100%).
  • Clustering: Detect unknown anomalous patterns.

Potential benefit

Protect millions of clients from financial losses, reduce operational costs of fraud investigation, improve customer experience by avoiding false blocks, and adapt to new scams that constantly evolve.
