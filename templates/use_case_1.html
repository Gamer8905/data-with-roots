Use Case 1

Medical Diagnosis with AI

Machine Learning application to detect diseases from medical images.

Problem Context

Early medical diagnosis is crucial for the effective treatment of diseases like skin cancer. Experienced dermatologists can identify malignant lesions, but the shortage of specialists in many regions delays diagnoses.

The problem is: How to automate early detection of malignant skin lesions from medical images, reducing diagnosis time and improving accuracy.

🏥

ML Type

Supervised Learning - Classification

Deep Learning CNN Transfer Learning

Data that could be used

  • Dermoscopic images of skin lesions.
  • Patient metadata (age, gender, history).
  • Extracted features: asymmetry, border, color, diameter (ABCD rule).
  • Confirmed histopathological diagnosis (benign/malignant).

What the model should predict

  • Classification: Whether the lesion is benign or malignant.
  • Probability: Confidence level of the diagnosis.
  • Specific type: Classify between melanoma, carcinoma, nevus, etc.

Potential benefit

Reduce diagnosis time from weeks to seconds, enable mass screenings in rural areas, assist dermatologists with an automated second opinion, and significantly increase early detection rates of skin cancer, saving lives.
