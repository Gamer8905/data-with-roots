Use Case 4

Autonomous Vehicles

ML for autonomous driving: perception, decision-making and control in real-time.

Problem Context

Traffic accidents cause more than 1.35 million deaths per year worldwide, and 94% are due to human error. Autonomous vehicles promise to revolutionize road safety through intelligent systems that can perceive, decide and act better than a human driver.

The problem is: How to make a vehicle navigate safely and autonomously in complex environments, recognizing objects, predicting behaviors and making decisions in real-time.

🚗

ML Type

Reinforcement Learning + Supervised

Computer Vision Sensor Fusion RL

Data that could be used

  • LiDAR sensors and 360-degree cameras.
  • GPS, IMU and speed data.
  • High-definition environment maps.
  • Real-time traffic data.
  • Expert human driver history.

What the model should predict

  • Detection: Identify pedestrians, vehicles, signs and obstacles.
  • Classification: Type and behavior of each detected object.
  • Decision: Optimal action: brake, accelerate, turn, change lane.

Potential benefit

Drastically reduce traffic accidents caused by human error, improve transportation efficiency by reducing congestion, offer mobility to elderly or disabled people, and optimize fuel or electricity consumption.
