Practical Application

Estimate Delivery Time based on Distance

Simple Linear Regression model implemented with scikit-learn, numpy, pandas, and matplotlib.

# 1. Problem Context

Problem to Solve

What problem is being solved?

Logistics and delivery companies need to accurately estimate how long a shipment will take in order to plan routes, assign couriers, and improve the customer experience.

The phenomenon being analyzed is the relationship between the distance a package travels from origin to destination and the total time it takes to complete the delivery.

The data represents 600 historical shipments with their distance traveled and recorded actual delivery time. Simple Linear Regression is applied to:

  • Discover the mathematical relationship between distance and time.
  • Predict the estimated delivery time for new distances.
  • Improve logistics planning with real data.
